## Hi there 👋

I'm a PhD student in biomedical engineering at McMaster University in the Human Neurophysiology and Imaging lab. My work focuses on developing and applying non-invasive closed-loop brain stimulation to improve cognition in people living with dementia. I work with electroencephalography (EEG) and transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS).
